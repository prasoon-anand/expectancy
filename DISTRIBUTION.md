# Distribution notice

Qualified Expectancy installers will be distributed without charge through the
official [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/prasoon-anand/expectancy/releases).
The first release targets macOS on Apple Silicon; Intel macOS, Windows and Linux
are planned later. Consult the [download catalogue](https://prasoon-anand.github.io/expectancy/downloads.html)
for actual availability. A planned platform is not a supported release.

Expectancy is proprietary software. The public repository provides installers,
release records, documentation, security information, and third-party notices; it
does not provide or license the application source code. Installation and use are
governed by the end-user terms included with the corresponding release.

Each release should be verified against the published target, version,
cryptographic digest, signing identity, dependency inventory, and known-issues
record before installation. Packages obtained from another location should not be
treated as an official Expectancy distribution without matching evidence.

Third-party libraries, runtimes, fonts, and other components retain their own
copyright and licence terms. Their target-specific notices accompany each
installer. User-supplied datasets and strategies remain subject to the rights and
restrictions of their respective owners.

This notice is descriptive and does not replace the end-user terms or
target-specific third-party notices supplied with a release.
