02 / A two-rule specification

Write the rule.
Separate the execution.

Enter when the 2-period simple moving average crosses above the 3-period average. Exit on the reverse crossing. Decisions use completed closes; orders fill at the next imported open.

  • Visual rules or a typed strategy document
  • Fixed quantity of 10 for this example
  • Commission and slippage stated before the run

Scientific scope

Historical evidence.
Not a forecast.

A backtest describes what a specified model produces on specified historical data. It does not establish future profitability or executable performance.

  • No supplied market data, brokerage connection or live orders.
  • Bar data cannot recover intraday paths, queue position or unobserved liquidity.
  • Numerically valid rows do not remove survivorship or selection bias.
